20 MB fixed maximum

Every announcement on this site is a printed plate: bone stock, a two-pixel ink rule, a forty-five degree corner, and a registration mark in each corner. Nothing is translucent, because a glass plate behind light type is a plate nobody can read.

Three plates,
one announcement

The reference stacks translucent glass panels and lets the light through. The stack is what carries the idea — the sequence, the overlap, the corner marks — so the stack is what was kept. The glass was not: each plate here is an opaque decision, and the ink on it is declared with it, so the pair cannot drift apart when a later stylesheet disagrees.

The audit reads every visible text node, composites whatever washes sit behind it, and fails the build below 4.5:1. That is why these three plates have no hairlines or ghost type over the copy.

Built from three references, audited after every change

The dial is the sliced instrument face; the plates are the announcement stack; the rings are the tunnel that recedes to a point. All three are drawn in CSS — no image was fetched, so nothing here can 404 and nothing needs a cache stamp.
